Privacy

Last updated 4 October 2026

This site has no accounts and no server of its own. In short: what you do here stays in your browser.

What is saved, and where

The following is stored in your browser’s local storage on this device, and nowhere else:

  • Your language, sound and music preferences.
  • Your stamp card: the date, length, seat, drink and task name of each focus session.
  • A focus session in progress, so a refresh does not lose it.

None of it is sent to us or to anyone else. Clearing this site’s data in your browser deletes all of it.

What other services see

  • Hosting. The site is served by GitHub Pages and the ambience audio by Cloudflare. Like any web host, they receive your IP address and browser details when files load.
  • YouTube station. This is optional and off until you turn its slider up. When you do, a player loads from YouTube’s privacy-enhanced domain (youtube-nocookie.com), and Google’s terms and privacy policy apply to that player. YouTube may show ads.
  • Visit counts. The site may use Cloudflare Web Analytics, which counts page views in aggregate without cookies and without identifying individual visitors.
  • Error reports. If the page hits a bug, a technical report (the error message, where in the code it happened, your browser version and the page address) is sent to Sentry so it can be fixed. It does not include your task names, your stamp card, or a record of what you clicked.

隐私说明

最后更新：2026 年 10 月 4 日

这个网站没有账号系统，也没有自己的服务器。一句话：你在这里做的事只留在你的浏览器里。

保存了什么，存在哪里

以下内容保存在这台设备的浏览器本地存储里，不会存到别处：

  • 语言、环境音和音乐的偏好。
  • 集点卡：每次专注的日期、时长、座位、饮品和任务名。
  • 进行中的专注，这样刷新页面不会丢。

这些都不会发送给我们或任何人。在浏览器里清除本站数据，就会全部删除。

其他服务能看到什么

  • 托管。网站由 GitHub Pages 提供，环境音由 Cloudflare 提供。和任何网站一样，加载文件时它们会收到你的 IP 地址和浏览器信息。
  • YouTube 电台。这是可选功能，在你把它的滑块拉起来之前不会加载。打开后，播放器从 YouTube 的隐私增强域名（youtube-nocookie.com）加载，适用 Google 的条款和隐私政策，YouTube 可能会播放广告。
  • 访问统计。网站可能使用 Cloudflare Web Analytics 统计总访问量，它不使用 cookie，也不识别具体访客。
  • 错误报告。页面出错时，会向 Sentry 发送一份技术报告（错误信息、出错的代码位置、浏览器版本和页面地址），用来修复问题。报告不包含你的任务名、集点卡，也不记录你点过什么。
